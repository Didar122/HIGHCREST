Project information
-------------------
- Project reference: ARC-2026-HC01
- Overall area: 1,000 m2 (40 m x 25 m)
- Main hall: 25 m x 25 m (625 m2)
- VIP cabin: 10 m x 25 m (250 m2)
- Service corridor and restrooms: 125 m2
- Guest capacity: 280 seated at tables or 450 in theatre layout
- Seating plan: 28 round tables for 8-10 guests
- Stage: 60 cm raised platform
- Building heights: 3 m at the eaves, 6 m at the roof apex
- Structure: 26 galvanized 150 mm x 150 mm steel columns, 6 mm thick
- Windows: 20 double-glazed arched windows
- Roof: 1,180 m2, three-layer PVDF membrane, 950 g/m2
- HVAC: Ducted packaged units, approximately 80-100 tons
